# SPC-0003 · Taste personalization, fresh discovery, and household progress sync

Tier: **feature** · Readiness: **67%**

Builds on: SPC-0001

Layers personalization and continuity onto the catalog and playback skeleton: non-repeating weighted-random hero selection, implicit and explicit genre affinity scoring, per-episode resume memory, automatic movie completion clearing, "Up Next" episode auto-advance, live broadcast schedule enrichment, and multi-device household state synchronization.

## Requirements

### REQ-0007 · Non-repeating weighted-random hero selection and genre taste personalization (provisional)

The system SHOULD learn the user's genre preferences from implicit and explicit interactions, record privacy-local launch and discovery-origin counters (`launches`, `heroOpens`, `pickedForYouOpens`, `otherOpens`, `searchOpens`, `rerolls`) on the active taste profile, and randomize home screen discovery on every launch so the same featured title is never repeated back-to-back:
1. **Taste profile scoring**: Track cumulative genre affinity (`+1` on detail modal open, `+3` on playback start, `+5` on title heart/like, `-4` on unlike, `+8` on pinned favorite genre chip, `-6` on unpin) plus up to `60` liked items and the last `12` shown hero IDs (`recentHeroIds`).
2. **Per-item personalization score**: `score = (voteAverage || 6.5) * 0.6 + sum(favoriteGenre ? 10 : 0) + sum(min(15, genreScore * 1.5)) + (isLiked ? 6 : 0)`.
3. **Weighted-random hero & carousels**: Exclude `recentHeroIds` and items without backdrop imagery, rank candidates by `score + random * 8`, pick from the top `8` candidates at random, and record the chosen hero ID in `recentHeroIds`. Provide a manual **"Surprise Me"** re-roll button, interactive genre filter chips, a **"Picked for You"** row, and a **"Your Liked Titles"** row.

**REQ-0007#1**

```gherkin
Given a user who opens the Home view three times in a row or clicks "Surprise Me"
When the hero banner selects a featured title from the candidate pool
Then each selection is excluded from the next 12 hero selections and no two consecutive opens show the same hero title
```

**REQ-0007#2**

```gherkin
Given a user who pins "Sci-Fi" and "Thriller" on the taste bar or likes a Sci-Fi movie
When the Home view renders
Then the "Picked for You" row and hero pool prioritize Sci-Fi and Thriller titles while keeping carousel ordering fresh via weighted random jitter
```

Because:
- FCT-0006 (active): Deterministic catalog ordering displays the exact same top-ranked title on the home hero banner on every session
- FCT-0013 (active): Weighted random ranking over a 12-item hero exclusion window prevents repeats across 30 launches and adapts immediately to pinned genres
- ASM-0002 (open): Heuristic genre affinity weights and a 12-item hero history window keep discovery fresh and personal

### REQ-0008 · Per-member household profiles, watch progress persistence, episode resume memory, and state sync (active)

The system MUST isolate viewer state per household member profile (`profileId`), support household profile customization (`nickname`, color swatch, deletion, and `isKids` Kids account mode), track playback progress across movies and series episodes, surface an actionable **"Continue Watching"** row for the active member, auto-advance episodes, and synchronize member state across devices on the home network:
1. **Per-member household state isolation & profile management**: Support switching, creating, renaming/nicknaming (`nickname` falling back to `name`), recoloring, deleting (while retaining at least one profile), and toggling `isKids` Kids mode on household member profiles in the navigation bar and profile manager without login credentials; each member profile maintains its own independent `watchProgress`, per-episode resume positions, `favoriteChannelIds`, and genre/liked `tasteProfile`.
2. **Kids account filtering (`isKids`)**: When an active profile has `isKids` enabled, filter Home rows, Hero picks, Search results (`Search Kids`), Taste Genres, Catalog genres/listings (`?kids=1`), and Live TV channels (`Kids` category) to age-appropriate titles carrying `Family`/`Kids`/`Children` or kid-safe anime genres (`Adventure`, `Comedy`, `Fantasy`, `Music`, `Slice of Life`, `Sports`, `Sci-Fi`, `Family`, `Kids` without `Action`/`Drama`), while excluding mature genres (`Horror`, `Crime`, `Thriller`, `War`, `Psychological`, `Mystery`, `Romance`), standalone documentaries (`Documentary`), and unrated adult animation lacking `Family`/`Kids`.
3. **Progress recording (`>= 5s`)**: Persist playback position and duration per member profile (up to `40` entries, stripping nested season trees to keep storage compact). Preserve late ending scenes (`< 97%`, such as `95%`), and automatically remove movies reaching `>= 97%` of duration from that member's Continue Watching row as completed (`FCT-0012`).
4. **Per-episode resume memory**: For series and anime, store a per-episode map keyed by `${season}:${episode}` so switching between episodes or seasons preserves each episode's exact resume timestamp and completion percentage bar (`>= 90%` marked watched).
5. **"Up Next" episode auto-advance**: During the final `30 seconds` of an episode (or upon `ended`), display an "Up Next" prompt with a `10-second` countdown that automatically launches the next episode in the season (or Season `N+1` Episode `1`).
6. **Household state sync (`/api/state?profile=<id>`)**: Synchronize each member's `watchProgress` (max `40`), `favoriteChannelIds` (max `200`), and the household member roster (`profiles` with `id`, `name`, optional `nickname`, `color`, and optional `isKids`) with a persistent server-side state store, applying atomic per-item timestamped upserts (`updatedAt`) and per-episode map merges on the server and on client startup.

Because:

- FCT-0009 (active): Per-member household profile partitioning with atomic timestamped upserts isolates viewer state across devices

### REQ-0009 · Live broadcast schedule and series air-date status enrichment (active)

The system MUST enrich series discovery and detail views with live broadcast schedule data:
1. **"What's On Today" row**: Aggregate series airing new episodes on the current date (resolved by IMDb ID or title lookup) and display them in a dedicated Home carousel with episode badges (`S2 · E5`).
2. **Series status badge**: In the detail modal for any series, display whether the show is returning or ended alongside the next scheduled episode (`Next: Sx · Ey · YYYY-MM-DD`) or most recent episode air date.


## Current Production Appliance Stack

Lumen compiles and ships as a single self-contained binary with the frontend bundle embedded directly into the executable (`//go:embed`).

| Layer | Technology | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Backend & HTTP Appliance** | **Go 1.24+** (`net/http`, `cmd/lumen`, `internal/server`) | Single-binary HTTP server, API router, static SPA asset server (`io/fs`), and SSRF-guarded outbound proxy. |
| **Frontend SPA** | **Vite 8** + **React 19** + **TypeScript 5** (`src/`) | Client-side single-page application compiled to `web/dist` and embedded into the Go binary via `web/embed.go`. |
| **Styling & Icons** | **Tailwind CSS 4** + **Lucide React** | Dark-mode glassmorphic UI, viewport-aware custom dropdowns, 4-layer playback timeline scrubber, and responsive layout. |
| **Specification Workspace UI** | **ASDLC Meta-DesignSystem** (`asdlc-meta-designsystem@latest`) | Zero-build Web Component Atoms, Molecules, Organisms, and Templates powering `site/`. |
| **BitTorrent Streaming Engine** | **`github.com/anacrolix/torrent`** (`internal/streaming`) | Real-time torrent swarm ingestion, custom piece prioritization (head/tail + dynamic readhead window), contiguous downloaded-range calculation, and 20-minute idle eviction. |
| **Media Remuxing & Transcoding** | **`ffmpeg` / `ffprobe`** (`internal/streaming`) | Runtime codec probing (`h264`, `hevc`, `aac`, `ac3`, `eac3`), zero-CPU fragmented MP4 (`fMP4`) copy-remuxing, and on-the-fly audio/video transcoding with `-ss` seek support. |
| **Live TV HLS Playback** | **`hls.js` 1.7** + Native `<video>` (`src/components/VideoPlayerModal.tsx`, `internal/live`) | Adaptive HLS live stream playback paired with `/api/live/proxy` manifest rewriting and CORS relay. |
| **Household State Persistence** | **`modernc.org/sqlite`** (`internal/state`) | Pure-Go (CGO-free) embedded SQLite database (`WAL` mode) storing isolated per-member household profiles (`name`, `nickname`, `color`, `is_kids`), watch progress, per-episode resume positions, and favorite live channels. |
| **Network Security Guard** | **`net.Dialer.Control`** (`internal/netguard`) | Socket-level IP validation at connection time to block RFC1918, loopback, link-local, CGNAT, and cloud metadata addresses and eliminate DNS-rebinding TOCTOU windows. |

## Directory Layout

- `cmd/lumen/main.go` — Single-binary entrypoint (`PORT`, `LUMEN_DATA_DIR`).
- `internal/server/` — HTTP handlers for `/api/health`, `/api/media/*` (including `?kids=1` filtering in `kids.go`), `/api/stream`, `/api/stream/status`, `/api/subtitles/*`, `/api/live/*`, and `/api/state`.
- `internal/metadata/` — Keyless Cinemeta, AniList GraphQL, `Fribb/anime-lists` cross-reference, and optional TMDB client with genre ID mapping.
- `internal/scrapers/` — Concurrent Torrentio, TPB (`apibay.org`), YTS, Nyaa/AnimeTosho, and custom Stremio addon scrapers with source ranking.
- `internal/streaming/` — `anacrolix/torrent` engine, piece prioritization, `downloadedRanges` telemetry, and `ffmpeg`/`ffprobe` remux/transcode pipeline.
- `internal/live/` — Free-TV/IPTV M3U parser with ISO country attribution & `Kids` classification, fallback broadcaster list, and HLS manifest/segment CORS proxy.
- `internal/subtitles/` — OpenSubtitles REST v3 lookup and SRT-to-WebVTT converter.
- `internal/state/` — Embedded SQLite per-member household state store (`profile_watch_progress`, `profile_favorite_channels`, `household_profiles`).
- `internal/personalization/` — Genre affinity scoring and row personalization.
- `internal/netguard/` — SSRF and DNS-rebinding socket-level dialer guard.
- `src/` — React 19 + TypeScript SPA source (`Navbar`, `HeroBanner`, `MediaCarousel`, `PosterCard`, `CatalogBrowser`, `MediaDetailModal`, `LiveChannelsGrid`, `VideoPlayerModal`, `SettingsModal`, `ProfileManagerModal`, `SelectDropdown`, `kidsFilter.ts`, `watchProgress.ts`).
- `web/` — `//go:embed all:dist` package embedding the production Vite bundle.
